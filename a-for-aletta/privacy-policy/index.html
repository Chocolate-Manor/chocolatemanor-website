A for Aletta – Privacy Policy

Effective 11 November 2026

Who we are

A for Aletta is made by Chocolate Manor, Bernardinopas 14, 5624 NA Eindhoven, Netherlands. Contact: haocheng.yang@chocolatemanor.org.

What we collect

Nothing. The game has no accounts, ads, analytics, tracking or online features, and no permission to use the internet.

Your saved game

Your progress is saved only on your device. We never receive it, and uninstalling the game deletes it. If you use Android backup, your device may include it in your own Google account backup.

Google Play

You download the game through Google Play, which is covered by Google's privacy policy.

Sharing

We share and sell nothing, because we hold nothing.

Children

The game collects nothing from anyone, including children.

Your rights

We hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing to access, correct or delete. Questions go to the email above. You can also complain to your local data protection authority; in the Netherlands, that is the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.

Changes

If anything changes, we will update this page and its date.
